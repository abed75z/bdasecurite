The fire safety service

Prevent, alert, respond.

Depending on their classification, many public venues (ERP) and all high-rise buildings (IGH) must have a fire safety and assistance to persons service, staffed by SSIAP-qualified officers.

The qualifications

SSIAP 1, 2 and 3: who does what?

SSIAP 1 · Officer

Prevention patrols, monitoring of the fire safety system, alarm checks, first response to an outbreak of fire, evacuation and welcoming the emergency services.

SSIAP 2 · Team leader

Supervises SSIAP 1 officers, runs the security control room, organises patrols and coordinates the response when an alarm goes off.

SSIAP 3 · Head of service

Manages the fire safety service, keeps track of regulations and safety commissions, advises the operator and keeps the registers.

24/7 posts

Continuous presence day and night, with a schedule agreed with you and replacements guaranteed in case of absence.
