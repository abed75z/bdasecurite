1212|BDA Équipe app and staff area
1213|(reserved for BDASECURITE staff): account name, first name, username, email and phone number; schedule, start and end of shifts; GPS position recorded only when the officer clocks in or out, scans a patrol checkpoint or triggers an SOS alert (never continuously); messages, incident reports and daily log entries written by the officer, with any photos they choose to attach; the device's notification token (Apple Push Notification service) to receive the schedule and messages from management. Purpose: organising work, time tracking, staff safety and reporting on the service to the client. Legal basis: performance of the employment contract and the employer's legal obligations. The client of a site sees the daily log of that site (times, events, first name and initial of the officer), never the photos or the officers' contact details. No data is sold or used for advertising, and the app contains no trackers. Data is stored on the website's server (OVH, France) and kept for the duration of the employment contract and then for the legal periods (5 years for payroll and working-time records). Officers can delete their account at any time from the app (Me › Settings).
