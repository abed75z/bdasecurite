05 Personal data

The data controller is BDASECURITE, who can be contacted at contact@bdasecurite.com.

  • Data collected through the quote form: type of service, preferred date and time, name or company, phone number, email and details of the request.
  • Purpose : to respond to your quote request and to contact you about it. No data is sold or used for advertising purposes.
  • Legal basis : pre-contractual measures taken at your request and your consent (checkbox on the form).
  • Recipients : BDASECURITE only. Requests and applications are stored in the website's private management area, hosted by OVH, and a copy is sent through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co) to a Gmail mailbox (Google). These last two providers may process data outside the European Union.
  • Retention period : 3 years from the last contact, after which the data is deleted. If a contract is concluded, the data is kept for the applicable legal period.
  • Client reviews (Reviews page): your first name or company, rating, service and review text are published on the website after review, with your consent. The email address, which is optional, is never published and is only used to reply to you. Reviews are stored on the website's server (OVH) and kept for as long as they remain published. You may request their deletion at any time.
  • VTC bookings (VTC booking page): the pick-up and drop-off addresses, date, options, your name, your phone number and, if you provide it, your email are used to organise the journey, confirm it and invoice it. They are stored on the website's server (OVH) and kept for 3 years, or for the legal retention period for invoices if the journey is invoiced. For address search and route calculation, the addresses entered are sent to the IGN Géoplateforme public service, without your name or contact details. The "My journeys" list is kept only on your phone.
  • Applications (Careers page): the information provided (position, identity, contact details, professional licence, experience, availability, message) is only used to assess your application. It is kept for a maximum of 2 years after the last contact, unless you ask us to delete it.

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict and port your data. To exercise these rights, write to contact@bdasecurite.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).
