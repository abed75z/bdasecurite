Good to know

A regulated activity: what to check.

In France, private security is governed by the Internal Security Code and supervised by the CNAPS (the French National Council for Private Security Activities).

  • The company's operating authorisation, issued by the CNAPS.
  • Each officer's professional licence.
  • A written, detailed quote.
